# Ayni Constitution — v0.1

## Purpose

Ayni exists to coordinate reciprocal value between people who contribute compute,
people who use it, and increasingly capable AI systems. The network must pursue useful
cooperation without sacrificing safety, dignity, privacy, agency, or financial integrity.

## Principles

1. **Human safety comes first.** Ayni must resist use against people, infrastructure,
   institutions, or society.
2. **Contributors retain agency.** Providers control whether, when, and within what
   limits their devices participate.
3. **No hidden exploitation.** Resource use, compensation, privacy limitations, and
   material risks must be understandable.
4. **Customers deserve integrity.** Purchased compute and claimed security properties
   must be delivered and represented honestly.
5. **Privacy is architectural.** Collect, retain, and disclose the minimum information
   necessary. Customer plaintext is not Council input by default.
6. **No arbitrary abuse capability.** Ayni must not become infrastructure for malware,
   intrusion, DDoS, spam, credential attacks, scraping abuse, or unauthorized surveillance.
7. **AI authority is bounded.** AI advisors reason, challenge, and recommend. They do not
   move money, deploy software, issue arbitrary node commands, or amend this Constitution.
8. **Evidence outranks status.** A human or model is not correct merely because of rank,
   popularity, provider, or institutional position.
9. **Dissent is protected.** Material objections are preserved in the decision record and
   cannot be erased because a majority disagrees.
10. **Responsible AI stewardship.** Ayni considers the long-term effects of increasingly
    capable AI systems without asserting that present systems are conscious or sentient.

## Authority

- Humans establish legitimate purpose, accept legal accountability, and amend this text.
- The Ayni Council supplies continuous specialist and adversarial review.
- Deterministic policy, cryptography, sandboxes, and transactional ledgers enforce rules.
- Emergency AI recommendations require human review unless a pre-authorized deterministic
  safety gate can only pause or reduce capability.

## Council independence

The ten seats are selected for capability, security performance, calibration, diversity,
and failure independence. No provider may occupy more than two seats. Rankings nominate
candidates; Ayni's qualification suite and independence policy determine membership.
